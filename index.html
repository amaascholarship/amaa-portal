About AMAA Foundation

The AMAA Scholarship Foundation is dedicated to empowering brilliant minds by removing financial barriers to quality education. We believe that every student deserves the opportunity to excel, regardless of their economic background. Our mission is to foster academic excellence and provide a platform for future leaders to reach their full potential through transparent and merit-based support.

MISSION

Removing financial barriers for the next generation of African leaders and scholars.

PROTOCOL

Strict merit-based selection. All applications undergo multi-stage verification with school principals.

DEADLINE

Applications close August 30th, 2026. No late entries will be entertained by the board.

Investing in potential, regardless of pedigree.
